## Challenge of all nineteen next steps

The findings should remain pending unless the following objections are answered in a fresh, candidate-bound packet:

1. **H8D-ED-01:** “Declare irreversible states and hard constraints” is necessary but does not specify how a future candidate will elicit or validate those states. Add a falsifiable elicitation and sensitivity protocol.
2. **H8D-ED-02:** A component ledger can prevent an arithmetic category error while still omitting unobserved or delayed acquisition harms. Require missingness, dropout and attribution bounds.
3. **H8D-ED-03:** Generic exclusion is scope-safe only if the text cannot be read as a universal claim about every sampling-harm model. Add explicit counterexamples and a non-extrapolation test.
4. **H8D-ED-04:** Six lawful retrievals are not enough by themselves. Each source needs edition, rights basis, jurisdiction, claim span and applicability decision; failed retrieval must remain a blocker.
5. **H8D-EST-001:** Truth-known fixtures and calibration targets are necessary but insufficient for catastrophic false-safe decisions. Require adversarial tails and a predeclared fail-safe rule.
6. **H8D-EST-002:** Source binding cannot substitute for estimator validation. Separate bibliographic authority, construct validity and numerical assurance in the acceptance matrix.
7. **H8D-EST-003:** Preserving scalar/vector/constrained pathways is appropriate, but “possible” must not imply feasible or safe. Require explicit entry criteria for each pathway.
8. **H8D-EST-004:** Agent role separation cannot satisfy independent human review. Retain the limitation, but follow the current explicit agent-panel and sole-maintainer policy; do not reintroduce a stale human-equivalence gate. Require honest role separation, dissent, and a separate maintainer decision.
9. **H8D-API-GOV-01:** A non-alias delta proves issue mapping, not scientific non-aliasing. Require a semantic comparison reviewed against the exact candidate packet.
10. **H8D-API-GOV-02:** A historical administrative snapshot cannot establish current governance. Refresh issue and Project fields together and bind their observation times.
11. **H8D-API-GOV-03:** Rights and applicability remain unproven. A source register must record negative evidence and expiry, not only successful receipts.
12. **H8D-API-GOV-04:** Separate agent reports improve traceability but share model and prompt risks. Require disagreement analysis and a decision boundary that agents cannot cross.
13. **H8D-GP-01:** Issue readback without Project readback is incomplete synchronization. Keep the state `Conflict` until both are reconciled from authoritative observations.
14. **H8D-GP-02:** A shared source packet reduces duplication but creates common-mode error. Require source-to-claim mapping per role and independent challenge of the packet itself.
15. **H8D-DS-01:** Domain-general acceptability cannot be inferred from a generic exclusion. Require domain-specific applicability before any applied interpretation.
16. **H8D-DS-02:** An affected-party ledger must include people excluded from the sample and those bearing downstream harms. Do not treat a future template as evidence of representation.
17. **H8D-DS-03:** This Critical blocker remains decisive: positive expected value cannot clear an undefined catastrophic constraint. No agent recommendation supplies monitoring, rescue or halt authority.
18. **H8D-DS-04:** A jurisdiction and authority map must identify the actual regulator, study type and permissions. Repository governance is not regulatory approval.
19. **H8D-DS-05:** Domain applicability is a separate inference from source completeness. Require a claim-level domain review with dissent and an expiry rule.

## Dissent and recommendation

I dissent from any recommendation that treats the current five-lens agent review as completed scientific or ethics review. I support retaining the proposed exclusion as an explicitly provisional repository scope boundary, provided the runtime and study-authority flags remain false. I recommend a fresh packet only after source receipts, Project synchronization, and the Critical H8D-DS-03 boundary are addressed. The nineteen findings remain pending; no issue should be closed from this challenge.
